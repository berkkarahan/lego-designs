# Mini Palet R1

First design, imported from the supplied Turkish 19-page [build guide](Mini_Palet_R1_Yapim_Plani.pdf). It contains 14 assembly steps and is explicitly a photo-based prototype, not a physically tested instruction set.

- [Required parts](parts.json): 17 requirement lines, 97 counted items including electronics/controller.
- [Stock comparison](inventory-check.json): two requirement lines covered by recorded stock; others need verification. Stock is not reserved.
- [Verification and next steps](VERIFICATION.md)

The guide uses two independently controlled tracks, one M-looking and one L-looking motor, a large battery box, receiver and remote. It specifies 18 wide links per side and 3L, 8L and 12L axles. These are design requirements, not proof the kits contain those exact axle sizes or compatible electronics.

Original PDF bytes are preserved; editable artwork was not supplied. Its internal v2.0 marking belongs to the original document; R1 is the model name. Do not regenerate or overwrite the original when revising the design. Save revisions in a named subfolder here and record their status.

Next: physically identify motors/receiver, count gear teeth, measure axle and pin lengths, check frame geometry and wheel/link fit, then test assembly and low-speed control. Do not mark ready to build until those dependencies are resolved.
